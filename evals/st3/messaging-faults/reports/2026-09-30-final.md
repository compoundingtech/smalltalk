## Failures today

**Channel killed:** the real extension opens a replacement channel, but the queued probe remains `sent` with zero native handoffs and zero read claims throughout the 25-second observation tail. The provider and runtime stay unchanged. st reports delivery as `current`. [Before](../evidence/2026-09-30-final/channel-killed/before.json), [after](../evidence/2026-09-30-final/channel-killed/after.json), [reader receipts](../evidence/2026-09-30-final/channel-killed/reader.json), [message trace](../evidence/2026-09-30-final/channel-killed/trace.json).

Code inspection explains this result: the extension reconnects through `open(ctx, true)` but does not run the idle proof used at session startup. A fresh channel starts with `first_idle_seen = false` and polls without forwarding messages until it receives an idle edge. The graph still holds the preceding channel's idle state, so polling makes the public delivery report current while queued mail cannot be handed off. This is an inference from the observed replacement process and the extension/channel code; the eval does not instruct the reader to check mail or synthesize an idle edge to recover it.

**Historical channel:** a real pre-reexec channel consumes one copy and records a graph read 758 ms after clearing, on the same provider and runtime. st nevertheless reports `stale`: "no delivery process has polled this seat's mailbox since the daemon started 25s ago". Its plain mailbox polls lack the delivery-report metadata required by the new daemon.
