## Evidence and coverage

The always-on [writer-hold reader](../../crates/st3/src/slo.rs) keeps 1m, 5m and 1h whole-hold histograms. Its holder table contains the 16 largest holders by total writer time over the last hour, each with count, over-100-ms count, total and maximum; it does **not** contain a per-holder p99. The `long_holds` ring keeps only the latest 64 over-target holds. It charges a physical batch to the job with the longest body, which does not prove that job alone caused the full hold. A lend is charged to its caller. Restarts clear these windows. The 24-hour `st slo chart` stores minute SLO remaining values, not hold samples or per-holder distributions. Thus 24-hour holder p99, maximum, over-target count and complete coverage are **UNKNOWN** on all three members. No member's window is pooled with another.
