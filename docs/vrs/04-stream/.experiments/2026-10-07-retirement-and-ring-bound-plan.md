# Stream request-retirement and ring-bound plan

Date: 2026-10-07

Status: plan; no replacement proof or ring measurement is claimed here.

This plan resolves the work needed for [DQ-S1, DQ-S3, and DQ-S4](../open-questions.md), without changing their unresolved status. The accepted direction is [decision 0004](../../.decisions/0004-stream-events-are-a-distinct-record-kind.md): typed service-principal requests are absorbed by stream events and ordinary `message reply`; reply status derives from `in-reply-to`. Retirement is conditional on a routable producer endpoint and proof, not on the decision alone (decision 0004, lines 57–62).

## A. Typed request/reply retirement

`src/request.rs` defines `RequestEnvelope`, `ReplyEnvelope`, and `PublicationRecord` with `deny_unknown_fields` (`src/request.rs`, lines 38–71). Do not extend these wire types or migrate their deduplication receipts. The accepted consequence is a fresh event/message namespace and retirement of request receipts, not an in-place wire migration (decision 0004, lines 91–96). The current **Idempotent service requests** invariant still names `tests/request_cli.rs` and remains load-bearing until replaced (`docs/vrs/.delta/DELTA-002-typed-request-absorption-pending.md`, lines 28–36).

| Gate | Required work and proof | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Keep compatibility intact | Leave `src/request.rs`, the `st2 request` surface, its request-state behavior, and the current invariant row unchanged while any later gate is incomplete. | No deprecation starts before every following prerequisite is met. |
| 1. Prove stream transport | Keep/add stream-ingress proofs for stable identity, concurrent/crash replay, changed-identity conflict, declared-recipient/fan-out behavior, and bounded retention. Existing ingress proofs include `tests/event_e2e.rs::stable_event_identity_publishes_exactly_one_canonical_message`, `::concurrent_replays_publish_exactly_one_event`, `::conflicting_reuse_and_undeclared_or_suspended_ingress_fail_closed`, and `::stream_state_is_bounded_and_forgets_only_beyond_its_honest_horizon`. | Replacement event publication guarantees are covered independently of the old request transport. |
| 2. Prove the reply endpoint (DQ-S1) | Define how an event's producer identity resolves to an authorized, real mailbox; a `<host>.<agent>/<stream>` `from` value alone is not a reply recipient. Add an end-to-end proof that an eval-owned external requester emits an event, the intended agent replies with ordinary `message reply`, and the reply is delivered to that requester's routable endpoint without an orphan mailbox or Agent Spec identity. Prove unknown/ambiguous endpoints and impersonation fail closed. | The endpoint is usable end to end by the external requester, preserving the compatibility capability in `MESSAGE-A01`; a grammar-only change is insufficient. |
| 3. Prove reply-derived status | Prove status is `pending` until an ordinary reply with the event's `in-reply-to` exists, then `replied` with that reply's sender/tags/body. Include missing, duplicate, and unrelated replies as applicable to the defined status contract. The status is derived from the ordinary message relation, not a second typed reply envelope (decision 0004, lines 57–60). | Callers can determine completion and retrieve the reply without `request-state/replies/`. |
| 4. Re-point invariant and start deprecation atomically | In one commit, replace the **Idempotent service requests** row's proof references with the complete stream publish + routable-reply + derived-status proof set, and announce the `st2 request` deprecation window. Keep the invariant-reference checker green. This ordering is required by the open drift record (`docs/vrs/.delta/DELTA-002-typed-request-absorption-pending.md`, lines 44–49). | The invariant no longer claims the typed path is the sole evidence, and users have a published migration window. |
| 5. Complete the window, then cut over | Keep the deprecated request entry points and explicit migration guidance available for one full release after the deprecation announcement. At the window's end, remove the request module/CLI and its request-state reads/writes; do not convert old receipts. Existing inbox records remain ordinary messages: users can read and reply through message commands. Delete the superseded typed-request spec sections, retain their forward pointers until the removal commit, and reword `MESSAGE-R11` to its surviving guarantee. Record the exact removal commit when closing DELTA-002. | No typed request wire reader/writer remains; no unsupported receipt migration is implied. |

If Gate 2 or 3 fails, stop the retirement sequence and keep the typed path and invariant row in force. The deprecation clock begins only at Gate 4, never when a plan or endpoint design is merged.

## B. Measure the stream receipt-ring bound (DQ-S3, STREAM-R04/R05)

The ring is the entire event-identity deduplication, conflicting-content-detection, and supersession-lookup horizon; an evicted identity is accepted as new without inbox/archive history scans (STREAM-R04/R05; `docs/vrs/04-stream/spec.md`, lines 172–186 and 263–267). Therefore evaluate K as a correctness horizon, not merely a cache hit-rate or speed setting. `128` is implemented but not yet measured (`docs/vrs/04-stream/spec.md`, lines 263–265).

### Collection protocol

1. **Choose representative sources and scope.** Collect per-stream, per-recipient observations from real adapter workloads covering CI transitions, build events, and timer sources. Include quiet periods, normal activity, bursts, retries, rediscovery after adapter restart, and delayed delivery/retry behavior. Record the observation interval and workload identity without recording event payloads or secrets.
2. **Capture the minimum fields.** For each attempted emit record a stable `(stream, event-id)` (or a privacy-preserving stable token), first-seen time, every retry/rediscovery time, whether the retry was a duplicate or a new identity, and the count of distinct event IDs admitted between the original event and its last retry. Also capture burst rate and event ordering. Do not infer the retry horizon from average emit rate alone.
3. **Cover tail behavior.** Run collection long enough to include the longest known retry/rediscovery schedule and representative source restarts; document gaps, censored retries, and any source whose retry policy is unknown. Separate observed maximum from percentile summaries; do not claim a finite guarantee from a sample that did not cover the relevant retry window.
4. **Replay against candidate K values.** For each captured sequence, simulate the current FIFO receipt-ring semantics. Report (a) distinct newer identities admitted before each retry, (b) whether that retry remains inside the ring, (c) duplicate/conflict decisions that would differ after eviction, and (d) maximum resident receipt count/state bytes. Include the implemented K=128 and candidate alternatives. Do not consult inbox/archive history in the simulation: that would violate the intended boundary.
5. **Check the decision threshold.** State the operational retry/rediscovery window the product intends to cover. To claim that a candidate K covers it, it must retain every identity through the last retry in the collected in-scope traces, with any uncaptured or unbounded retry policy explicitly listed as a limitation. If 128 fails, quantify the smallest fixed ring that covers the chosen window and its state cost; consider another bounded index only if a fixed ring is materially impractical. If 128 covers traces, retain it only with the observed workload, coverage limits, and the chosen safety margin documented.
6. **Publish evidence and resolve.** Add a results entry to this experiment plan (or a dated companion `.experiments/` report) with source/workload, collection dates, sample counts, retry-window coverage, replay method, measured maxima/distribution, state-size impact, limitations, and recommendation. Resolve DQ-S3 only after this report justifies keeping 128, changing K, or replacing the ring with a named bounded structure. Then update the spec's K statement and this question's status in the same documentation change.
